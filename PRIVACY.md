# JobRun Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 24 October 2026_

JobRun is a Chrome extension that helps you keep track of the jobs you save and apply to. This policy explains what information JobRun handles and what happens to it.

**In short: everything JobRun stores stays in your browser. JobRun has no servers, no accounts and no analytics, and it never sends your data to us or to anyone else.**

## What JobRun reads

JobRun reads a small amount of information from web pages, only to track your applications:

- **When you click "Save this page"** in the JobRun popup: the page's address (URL), its title, and the job title and company name shown on it.
- **On supported job application sites** (Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, Workday, SmartRecruiters and LinkedIn job pages): the page's address, the job title and company name, whether you clicked an Apply or Submit button, and whether a confirmation message such as "Application submitted" appears. JobRun uses this to offer to track a job and to move it to Applied once your application goes through.

JobRun does not read form fields, so it never sees what you type into an application, including your name, contact details, résumé or answers. It does not record your keystrokes, mouse movements or browsing on other sites.

## What JobRun stores, and where

All of it is kept locally in your browser using Chrome's extension storage:

- **Your job list:** for each job, its URL, job title, company name, stage (Saved, Applied, Interviewing, Offer or Denied) and the date you added it.
- **Applications in progress:** while you are applying in a tab, JobRun briefly remembers that job's address so it can recognise the confirmation page. This is cleared when you close the tab or the browser, and it expires after two hours.
- **Your theme preference:** light, dark or automatic.

## What JobRun shares

Nothing. JobRun does not send your data to the developer or to any third party, does not sell or transfer it, and does not use it for advertising, profiling, creditworthiness or any purpose other than tracking your job applications.

The only network request JobRun makes is for the typefaces in its popup, which load from Google Fonts. As with any web font, this means your browser requests the font files from Google. No information about you or your jobs is included in that request.

## Exporting and deleting your data

- **Export:** the "Export" button in the popup downloads your job list as a file on your computer. What you do with that file is up to you.
- **Delete a job:** use the delete button next to it in the popup.
- **Delete everything:** uninstall JobRun. Chrome removes all of its stored data.

## Permissions

- **storage:** to keep your job list and preferences in your browser.
- **activeTab and scripting:** to read the job title and company from the current page when you click "Save this page".
- **tabs:** to read the current tab's address and title so a job can be saved and matched to your list.
- **Access to supported job sites:** to recognise when you open or submit an application on those sites and show a small notification there.

## Changes to this policy

If JobRun's handling of data changes, this page will be updated and the date at the top changed.
